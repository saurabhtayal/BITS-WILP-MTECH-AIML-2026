The whole idea. A continuous piecewise-linear function with bends at c₁…cn can be written as a sum of ReLUs, where each weight is the slope jump at that bend. So a single hidden ReLU layer is a bend factory: enough hinges, placed densely, trace any smooth curve as closely as you like. That is the universal approximation theorem, made visible.
